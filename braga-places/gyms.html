Erasmus Local Tip

Avoid signing 12-month contracts! Always ask for no-fidelity student plans (sem fidelização). Also, check out the UMinho Sports Complex on Gualtar Campus — they offer the cheapest monthly gym and pool memberships in Braga for university students!
