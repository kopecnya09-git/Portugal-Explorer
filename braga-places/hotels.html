Erasmus Accommodation Tip

If you need short-term accommodation when arriving in Braga before moving into long-term student housing, Basic Braga by Axis is super convenient right by the train station. For family visits, central hotels like Vila Galé or INNSiDE by Meliá offer high comfort in the heart of the city.
