Travel Tips for Students

Local Food

  • Francesinha – the famous meat, cheese and beer-sauce sandwich from Porto (also great in Braga).
  • Pastel de nata – custard tart, usually around €1–2 in a local pastelaria.
  • Bifana – marinated pork sandwich, a classic cheap lunch.
  • Prato do Dia – dish of the day at a tasca, often a full meal for €7–10.

Transportation

  • Lisbon: the Navegante pass covers metro, buses and trains to Sintra and Cascais.
  • Porto: use the Andante card for metro, buses and urban trains.
  • Between cities: CP trains and long-distance buses connect Braga, Porto and Lisbon.
  • Late at night: Uber and Bolt work in all three cities.

About Portugal Explorer

Portugal Explorer is a foreign student's guide to Portugal. It was created by Erasmus students as part of an internship project, mentored by Carlos and the Web Team.

We cover Braga, Lisbon and Porto with the places we actually use as students: sights, food, nightlife, gyms, beaches and day trips, plus the practical tips we wish we had on day one.
